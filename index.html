Latest Food Trends

African Cuisine Goes Global

From Afrobeats to Afro-cuisine — restaurants across London, New York, and Paris are putting West African flavours on the map. Dishes like suya and egusi soup are appearing on fine-dining menus worldwide.

The Rise of Fermented Foods

Fermented staples like injera, dawadawa, and ogi are gaining recognition for their probiotic benefits and deep umami flavour — a tradition Africa has practised for centuries.

Ancient Grains Rediscovered

Grains like teff, fonio, and sorghum are being celebrated globally as nutritious, gluten-friendly alternatives with deep roots in African agriculture.
