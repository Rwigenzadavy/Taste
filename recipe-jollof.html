Jollof Rice

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • 400g canned tomatoes
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 2 scotch bonnet peppers (adjust to taste)
  • 1 large onion, divided
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

Show Cooking Instructions
  1. Blend tomatoes, red peppers, scotch bonnet, and half the onion into a smooth purée.
  2. Heat oil in a heavy-bottomed pot. Fry the remaining sliced onion until golden.
  3. Add the blended pepper mix and fry on medium heat for 20–25 minutes, stirring often, until the raw smell is gone and the sauce darkens.
  4. Stir in thyme, curry powder, and salt. Add stock and bring to a boil.
  5. Wash the rice and add it to the pot. Stir once, then reduce heat to low.
  6. Cover tightly and cook for 25–30 minutes until rice is tender and has absorbed the sauce.
  7. For the signature smoky "party jollof" flavour, allow the bottom to catch slightly before serving.
